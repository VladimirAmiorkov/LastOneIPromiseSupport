Delete your data

Last One, I Promise does not have user accounts, so there is no account to close. All of your data lives on your device and in any backup files you exported. We never hold a copy, so you can remove all of it yourself at any time using the steps below.

Delete a single watch

Open the watch in the Collection tab and delete it. Its wear history, accuracy and service entries, notes, photos and per-watch reports are deleted with it.

Delete everything

1. Delete the app from each device

  1. Touch and hold the app icon on the Home Screen.
  2. Tap Remove App, then Delete App.

Deleting the app removes all data stored on that device.

2. Delete any backup files you made

If you exported backups with Settings ▸ Local Backup & Restore, delete those files in the Files app.

Health data

The App only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it, so deleting the App does not change anything in the Health app. To revoke access, open the iOS Settings app ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Health ▸ Last One, I Promise.

Purchases

Purchase history is kept by Apple and is not affected by deleting the App. Cancel an active subscription in iOS Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions before deleting the App if you do not want it to renew.
