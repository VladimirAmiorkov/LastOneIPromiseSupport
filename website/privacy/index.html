Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
  • Your collection, history and Apple Health information are stored only on your device. The App never uploads them to iCloud or to us. We (the developer) cannot see or access any of it.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across apps and websites.
  • You can delete everything at any time. See Delete your data.

Who we are

"Last One, I Promise" (the "App") is developed by Vladimir Amiorkov ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to lastoneipromise.app@proton.me.

Data you enter

The App stores the information you choose to enter, such as your watches and their details (brand, model, specifications, purchase and ownership details), photos of your watches, the days you wore each watch, accuracy measurements, service history, notes and tags, and the reports the App generates from them.

This data is stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework. The App does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. If you export a backup with Settings ▸ Local Backup & Restore, the backup file is saved wherever you choose in the Files app and stays under your control. Like all apps, the App's data is included in your device's own iPhone or iPad backup (for example iCloud Backup) if you have turned that on in iOS.

Data the App reads with your permission

Each of the following is optional. The App asks before using it, and you can revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

PermissionWhat is usedWhy
Apple Health Steps, workouts, walking and running distance, cycling distance and flights climbed (read only) To show activity insights alongside the watch you wore. The App never writes to Apple Health.
Location (while using) Your approximate position at the moment of use To show local weather and to save the city and country where you wore a watch. Exact coordinates are never stored.
Motion & Fitness Motion activity on the device To detect the start of your day and remind you to log your watch. Processed on the device only.
Camera & Photos Photos you take or pick To add photos to your collection and to save images you create (such as rankings) to your library.
Notifications — To deliver winding, accuracy, service and report reminders. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Health information is used only to provide features inside the App. It is stored only on your device; the App never uploads it to iCloud or anywhere else. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

Services provided by Apple

  • WeatherKit — to show weather, your location is sent to Apple's weather service. Apple does not associate it with your identity.
  • App Store / StoreKit — purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details; we only learn whether a purchase is active.
  • Apple Intelligence — where available, AI-generated content (such as fun facts) is produced by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to other AI providers.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Live Activities — handled by iOS on your device.

Other network requests

If you enter a price in a currency other than your preferred one, the App downloads public currency exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). This request contains no information about you or your collection. As with any internet request, the provider can see your IP address.

Analytics and diagnostics

The App records usage events (for example, that a watch was marked as worn) to help us improve it. In the current version these events are written only to your device's system log and are not sent anywhere. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and analytics will not be linked to your identity.

If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and diagnostics with us, according to Apple's own policy.

Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. When you use Settings ▸ Contact Support in the App, the email also includes your app version, iOS version and iPhone model, which you can see and remove before sending. We use this only to answer you and fix problems you report, keep it only as long as needed for that, and never share or sell it. Email is handled by our email provider, Proton Mail.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or create accounts.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a watch in the App deletes its wear history, accuracy and service entries, notes, photos and per-watch reports. To remove everything, including any backup files you exported, follow the steps on Delete your data. We never hold a copy of the data you enter in the App, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To have your support emails deleted, email lastoneipromise.app@proton.me.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Because your data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the App: edit any entry, export everything with Settings ▸ Local Backup & Restore, or delete it as described above. If you need help, email lastoneipromise.app@proton.me.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
